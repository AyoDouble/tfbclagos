First Timer?

What to Expect
on Your First Visit

We want your first visit to feel as warm and easy as possible. Here's everything you need to know before you arrive.

1

Come as you are

There's no dress code requirement — come dressed comfortably. Most members dress in smart-casual or traditional Nigerian attire.

2

Arrive a little early

We recommend arriving 10–15 minutes before service begins so you can settle in and be welcomed by our ushers.

3

Your children are safe with us

We have a dedicated, supervised Children's Church that runs simultaneously with the main service — age 3 to 12 years.

4

Meet someone after service

Our Welcoming Team will be watching out for first-timers. Don't be shy — introduce yourself, and we'll help connect you to the community.

Our Story

A Church Built on
Faith & Family

The First Baptist Church, Lagos — lovingly known as TFBC Lagos — is a vibrant Christian community rooted in the timeless truth of God's Word. We are more than a church; we are a family.

Our doors are open to all — the seeker, the believer, the struggler, and the overcomer. Come as you are, and discover a community that will walk alongside you in every season of life.

Word-Centered

Every message rooted in scripture, guiding lives with truth.

Spirit-Led

Empowered by the Holy Spirit for authentic worship.

Family-Focused

Building strong bonds in Christ, one family at a time.

Community-Driven

Serving Lagos and beyond with love and compassion.

Our Ministries

Find your place and purpose in the body of Christ through one of our vibrant ministry arms.

Children's Ministry

A safe, fun, and Bible-centred environment where children ages 3–12 discover God's love through worship, stories, and activities.

Youth Ministry

Empowering teenagers and young adults to live boldly for Christ through fellowship, mentorship, and impactful outreach.

Men's Fellowship

Building godly men of character through Bible study, accountability, and servant leadership within the home and church.

Women's Ministry

A sisterhood of faith — nurturing women to walk in their God-given calling through prayer, mentorship, and community.

Choir & Worship

Spirit-filled musicians and singers who lead the congregation into the presence of God through anointed worship every service.

Outreach & Missions

Extending the love of Christ beyond our walls through community service, evangelism, and support for the underprivileged in Lagos.

Intercession

Submit a Prayer Request

We believe in the power of prayer. Our prayer team intercedes faithfully for every request submitted to us — nothing is too big or too small for God.

"Is anyone among you sick? Let them call the elders of the church to pray over them..." — James 5:14

Completely confidential. Your prayer request is handled with care and privacy by our dedicated prayer team.

Prayed over by a team. Multiple intercessors will lift your request before God each week.

Follow-up available. You may request to be followed up with after your prayer is submitted.

Share Your Request

We honour your trust. Every request is treated with love and discretion.
